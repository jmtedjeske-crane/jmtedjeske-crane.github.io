Authority Table Builder

Use this form to build your Step 7 authority table. Add one entry for each authority that you chose for your final work product, and mark each entry as case authority or other authority. You may choose no more than six authorities. The limit of six covers case authority and other authority together. The limit applies only to this table, not to your memo. When you finish, click Copy table and paste it into the text box of the Canvas assignment Text Box or Upload for Authority Table (Step 7). In the Canvas text box, your entries paste as two tables: one for case authority and one for other authority. This form is optional, and you use it at your own risk. You can also click Download as Word to save both tables in a Word document named Step7_AuthorityTable.docx, and upload that file to the same assignment. Or you can build the tables directly in the Canvas text box, or in your own Word or Excel file, instead.

Reminders
  • Do not put your name anywhere in this form.
  • Read or skim every authority on your master list of potentially relevant sources. Then choose no more than six authorities, and read in full every authority that you put in the table that you are submitting.
  • You may not use AI in Step 7, except for any additional research that you find you need.
  • Validate every case and every statute, court rule, constitutional provision, or regulation with a citator, and record which citator you used and what it showed.
  • If you add or drop authority while you draft in Step 8, you do not have to come back and change this table.
