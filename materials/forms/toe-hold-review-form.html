Use this form to record your first reading of each AI output in Step 4 and your review of a secondary source in Step 5, and to draft your Step 5 comparison essay. You write these reviews:

  • One review for each general AI tool that you used in Step 2 (Reviews A.1 to A.3).
  • One review for the [legal AI tool 1] output from Step 3 (Review B).
  • One review for the [legal AI tool 2] output from Step 3 (Review C).
  • One review for the [optional legal AI tool] output from Step 3 (Review D), only if you ran your Step 3 prompt in that tool.

When you finish, copy your work and paste it into the text box of the Canvas assignment Text Box for Toe-Hold Reviews, Secondary Source Review, and Comparison Essay (Steps 4 and 5). This form is optional, and you use it at your own risk. You can also type your work directly into the Canvas text box instead.

Reminders
  • Write each review before you verify anything. At this stage, you assume that the information in the AI output is complete and correct. You are not trying to verify every authority and every legal and factual assertion in the AI output.
  • More is not necessarily better. List a case or other authority only when it looks important.
  • This form uses no AI, because Steps 4 and 5 do not allow AI for the reviews or the comparison essay. In Step 5, you may use AI only to help you select the secondary source.

Step 4: Toe-Hold Reviews

Write one review for each AI output. Each review has four parts: Conclusion; Statutes, Court Rules, Constitutional Provisions, and Regulations; Cases; and Secondary Sources.

Step 5: Secondary Source

After you review what you learned from the AI outputs, research the issue in one traditional secondary source, such as a treatise, a practice guide, a legal encyclopedia, an ALR annotation, or a law review article. You may use more than one section of the source. You must read the source itself, because an AI tool’s summary of a source does not count as the source. Then complete Review E and the comparison essay below.

Step 5: Comparison Essay

Explain how well your AI outputs and the secondary source fit together. Answer all three questions below. Together, your answers can run no more than 300 words.
