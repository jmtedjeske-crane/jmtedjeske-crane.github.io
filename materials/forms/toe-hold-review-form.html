Toe-Hold Review and Comparison Essay Form

Use this form to record your first reading of each AI output in Step 4 and your review of a secondary source in Step 5, and to draft your Step 5 comparison essay. You write these reviews:

  • One review for each general AI tool that you used in Step 2 (Reviews A.1 to A.3).
  • One review for the [legal AI tool 1] output from Step 3 (Review B).
  • One review for the [legal AI tool 2] output from Step 3 (Review C).
  • One review for the [optional legal AI tool] output from Step 3 (Review D), only if you ran your Step 3 prompt in that tool.

When you finish, copy your work from the box at the bottom of this form, and paste it into the text box of the Canvas assignment Text Box for Toe-Hold Reviews, Secondary Source Review, and Comparison Essay (Steps 4 and 5). This form is optional, and you use it at your own risk. You can also type your work directly into the Canvas text box instead.

Reminders
  • Write each review before you verify anything. At this stage, you assume that the information in the AI output is complete and correct. You are not trying to verify every authority and every legal and factual assertion in the AI output.
  • More is not necessarily better. List a case or other authority only when it looks important.
  • You may not use AI to write the toe-hold reviews, but you may copy and paste text from the AI reports themselves. Clean up any text that you paste so that it answers the part of the review where you put it, and summarize longer passages instead of pasting them whole.
  • If a report does not give a complete citation, use the citation information that the report gives, or click through to the source to find the full citation. Keep your click-throughs quick, and spend most of your time examining the report itself, because you will review the underlying materials later.
  • You do not need to use Bluebook form in the toe-hold reviews.
  • This form uses no AI, because Steps 4 and 5 do not allow AI for the reviews or the comparison essay. In Step 5, you may use AI only to help you select the secondary source.
  • After you finish your reviews, you may use the Optional AI Formatting Prompt at the bottom of this form to clean up the formatting of your reviews. The prompt is optional, not required.

Step 4: Toe-Hold Reviews

Write one review for each AI output. Each review has four parts: Conclusion; Statutes, Court Rules, Constitutional Provisions, and Regulations; Cases; and Secondary Sources.

Step 5: Secondary Source

After you review what you learned from the AI outputs, research the issue in one traditional secondary source, such as a treatise, a practice guide, a legal encyclopedia, an ALR annotation, or a law review article. You may use more than one section of the source. You must read the source itself, because an AI tool’s summary of a source does not count as the source. Then complete Review E and the comparison essay below.

Optional AI Formatting Prompt

Optional

This prompt asks an AI tool to clean up the formatting of your toe-hold reviews and your Review E. The AI tool puts each review under a consistent heading, puts your cases in a table with your Why it matters labels and your short descriptions, marks blank entries, and corrects obvious errors in spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and spacing. The AI tool copies your secondary sources exactly as you wrote them. The AI tool then lists every change it made and every gap it found.

This prompt is optional, not required. You do not need to use the prompt, and you lose no credit if you skip it.
  • Use the prompt only after you write all of your reviews yourself. The prompt changes formatting only. The prompt does not write your reviews or your comparison essay, and you must write both without AI.
  • You remain responsible for everything that you submit. Edit the AI output, and review it against your own reviews line by line before you submit it. The AI tool can change a citation, drop a case or a reason label that you checked, or reword a conclusion, even though the prompt tells the tool not to.
  • The prompt does not verify anything. A wrong citation or a wrong description in your review stays wrong after formatting. You verify the authorities yourself in Step 6.
  • Do not submit the Formatting notes that the AI tool adds at the end of its output. Read the notes yourself, fix in your own work any problem that they list, and delete the notes before you submit.

To use the prompt, follow these four steps.
