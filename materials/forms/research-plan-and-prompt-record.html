Research Plan and Prompt Record

Use this form to draft your Step 1 research plan and to record the prompts that you run in Steps 2 and 3. When you finish, copy your work and paste it into the text box of the Canvas assignment Text Box for Research Plan (Step 1) and Record of Prompts for Steps 2 and 3. This form is optional, and you use it at your own risk. You can also type your answers directly into the Canvas text box instead.

Reminders
  • Complete the Step 1 research plan before you open any research tool. You may not use AI in Step 1.
  • Write the Step 2 and Step 3 prompts yourself, without AI. You must use AI to run them.
  • In every step, including the steps that do not allow AI, you may use the basic level of Grammarly or a similar writing tool to fix word choice, grammar, and typos.
This form does not save your work. Your text stays in this browser tab only, and nothing goes to Canvas or to your instructor.
