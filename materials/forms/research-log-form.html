Research Log Form

Use this form for Option A of Step 6. The form helps you record each search as you run it and then write your stopping statement. For each search or technique, the log records five things: the platform, the resource or technique, the authority that you started from, what you found, and whether you added any authority to your list.

When you finish, copy your work and paste it into the text box of the Canvas assignment Text Box or Upload for Research Log or Essay (Step 6), or download your log as a Word table and upload the Word file to that assignment. This form is optional, and you use it at your own risk.

This form is not a required format. If you already have your own way of keeping a research log, such as a notebook, a spreadsheet, or a document in your own layout, you may use your own log instead. Your log only needs to give your instructor enough information to see what you did. If you choose Option B, the descriptive essay, you do not need this form.

Reminders
  • Record every search, including a search that found nothing new.
  • The log has no word limit. The stopping statement can run no more than 100 words.
  • You may use AI for the research in Step 6, but you may not use AI to write the log or the stopping statement. This form uses no AI.
This form does not save your work. Your text stays in this browser tab only, and nothing goes to Canvas or to your instructor. If you research over more than one session, copy your log into a Word document or into the Canvas text box at the end of each session. You can also click Download as Word to save your work to your computer as a Word document named Step6_ResearchLog.docx. The Word document puts your log in a table and adds your stopping statement below the table.
Copy your work before you close this tab. You cannot recover text after the tab closes.
