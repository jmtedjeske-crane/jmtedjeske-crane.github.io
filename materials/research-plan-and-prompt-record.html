Steps 1 to 3: Research Plan and Prompt Record

Students use this optional form to draft the research plan for Step 1 of the Final Capstone Project and to record each prompt that they run in Steps 2 and 3. When students finish, they copy the text and paste it into the Canvas text box for those steps, or they download their work as a Word document.

The form runs entirely in the student’s browser. It does not save the student’s work, and it sends nothing to the instructor or to anyone else, so students must copy or download their work before they close the browser tab. The form is optional: students may also type their work directly into the learning management system.

Make Your Own Version

Back to top

The form is one HTML file, so you can adapt it for your own course. Download the file, open it in a text editor, and change the course-specific wording, such as the assignment name, the list of AI tools, and the number of prompts that students may run. Then post the file where your students can open it, for example on GitHub Pages, on your own website, or as a file in your learning management system. In some learning management systems, an HTML file works only after the student opens it in a new browser tab.

Before you share your version with students, test it: fill in each part of the form, copy the text, and paste it into your learning management system to confirm that every part arrives.
